# Invoice Reconciliation Policy

**Owner:** Finance Operations
**Applies To:** Contract manufacturing, supplier services, quality labs, logistics, and regulated materials
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-01

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how supplier invoices are reviewed before approval, dispute, recovery, or escalation. The goal is to prevent payment leakage while maintaining accurate and timely supplier payments.

## 2. Required reconciliation

Supplier invoices must be reconciled against all applicable evidence:

1. Contract terms and pricing schedules.
2. Purchase orders and approved change authorizations.
3. Receipts, shipment records, and delivered quantity.
4. Batch records and released quantity.
5. Quality release status and test evidence.
6. Milestone approval and deliverable acceptance.
7. Material consumption records and approved unit costs.
8. Capacity reservation and utilization records.

## 3. Decision states

Invoices and invoice lines must be assigned one of the following states:

- **Matched:** Evidence supports payment.
- **Variance:** Evidence shows a discrepancy that requires review.
- **Disputed:** The company should withhold or recover the disputed amount.
- **Escalated:** The finding has quality, compliance, IP, or supplier-governance risk.

## 4. Minimum evidence for approval

An invoice line may be approved only when the billed supplier, product, SKU, batch, milestone, material, capacity period, or shipment can be tied to an authorized source document.

## 5. Payment leakage categories

Common leakage categories include duplicate billing, off-contract fees, wrong rate, wrong volume, missed discount, invalid true-up, early milestone billing, unreleased batch billing, and unsupported pass-through charges.

## 6. Human review

Human approval is required for disputed amounts, escalated findings, exceptions involving rejected batches, and findings involving sensitive product or IP context.
