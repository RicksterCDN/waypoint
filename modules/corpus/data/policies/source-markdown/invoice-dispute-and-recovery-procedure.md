# Invoice Dispute and Recovery Procedure

**Owner:** Finance Operations and Procurement
**Applies To:** Supplier invoice variances and disputed amounts
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-01

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how invoice discrepancies are reviewed, disputed, recovered, and closed.

## 2. Dispute packet

Each dispute packet must include:

1. Supplier name and supplier ID.
2. Invoice number and invoice line.
3. Disputed amount and currency.
4. Contract clause or policy basis.
5. Supporting operational evidence.
6. Recommended supplier action.

## 3. Supplier response

Supplier communications must be specific and evidence-backed. The response should identify the invoice line, cite the controlling contract or policy term, explain the variance, and request correction, credit, backup documentation, or withdrawal of the charge.

## 4. Recovery states

Recovery opportunities move through these states:

- **Detected**
- **Triaged**
- **Assigned**
- **Supplier notified**
- **Credit requested**
- **Credit received**
- **Approved for payment**
- **Closed no recovery**

## 5. Withholding disputed amounts

The company may withhold disputed amounts while paying undisputed amounts according to payment terms. Withheld amounts must be reviewed at least weekly until resolved.

## 6. Escalation

Escalate disputes involving repeated supplier behavior, quality status conflicts, sensitive product information, IP-sensitive process terms, or potential compliance impact.
