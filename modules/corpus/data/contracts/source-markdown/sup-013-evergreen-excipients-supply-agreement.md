# Materials Supply Agreement: Evergreen Excipients

**Supplier ID:** sup-013
**Supplier:** Evergreen Excipients
**Document Type:** Regulated excipient supply and documentation agreement
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER excipient and specialty ingredient supply

## 1. Scope of services

Evergreen Excipients will supply regulated excipient lots, specialty ingredients, standard quality documentation, and approved rush-order fulfillment for sponsor manufacturing programs.

## 2. Unit cost and escalation

Excipient lot charges must use the contract unit cost in effect on the purchase order date. Annual material price escalation is capped at 2.5% unless the sponsor procurement lead approves a higher escalation in writing before shipment.

## 3. Material surcharge

Material surcharge fees are billable only for sponsor-approved raw material market events and must be calculated against the affected lot quantity. General freight, supplier overhead, or currency movement may not be billed as a material surcharge.

## 4. Rush order approval

Rush-order fees are billable only when the purchase order or written sponsor approval identifies the lot, requested ship date, and rush service level. Supplier-initiated schedule recovery is not billable as a rush order.

## 5. Included quality documentation

Standard certificate of analysis, material safety data sheet, and lot traceability documentation are included in the unit cost. Additional quality documentation is billable only when the sponsor requests a non-standard document set.
