# Packaging and Serialization Agreement: HelioPack Pharma Services

**Supplier ID:** sup-003
**Supplier:** HelioPack Pharma Services
**Document Type:** Packaging, serialization, and logistics billing agreement
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER-10 EU carton, ALLER-20 EU carton

## 1. Scope of services

HelioPack Pharma Services will provide secondary packaging, serialization, labeling, cartoning, and approved outbound logistics support for sponsor-authorized SKUs.

## 2. SKU and label authorization

Serialization, labeling, and cartoning charges are billable only for SKUs listed on the purchase order or in a sponsor-approved label change authorization. Relabeling for market, language, or artwork changes is billable only when the authorization identifies the affected SKU, market, and effective label version.

## 3. Serialized unit counts

Serialized-unit fees must reconcile to the lesser of the commissioned serial number count and the sponsor receipt record for the shipment. Units rejected during final packaging inspection may not be billed as serialized accepted units.

## 4. Logistics charges

Standard outbound logistics within the approved EU distribution lanes are included in the cartoning price. Premium freight, fuel surcharge, or alternate-carrier fees are billable only when the purchase order identifies the shipment ID and approved lane exception.

## 5. Invoice evidence

Each invoice must reference the SKU, packaging order, label version, shipment ID, serialized unit count, and any sponsor authorization used to support relabeling or premium logistics charges.
