# Clinical Supply Services Agreement: Orchid Clinical Supply

**Supplier ID:** sup-007
**Supplier:** Orchid Clinical Supply
**Document Type:** Clinical trial packaging and comparator sourcing agreement
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER Phase II clinical supply kits

## 1. Scope of services

Orchid Clinical Supply will provide clinical kit assembly, comparator sourcing support, labeled kit release, temperature-controlled storage, and study milestone documentation.

## 2. Kit assembly and release

Kit assembly fees are billable only for kits assembled under the approved study protocol and released for sponsor distribution. Destroyed, quarantined, or protocol-deviating kits may not be invoiced as released kits unless the sponsor clinical supply lead approves rework in writing.

## 3. Comparator pass-through

Comparator material is reimbursable at actual acquisition cost plus a 2% handling fee. Orchid must provide the supplier receipt, lot number, quantity, and study authorization before comparator pass-through charges are payable.

## 4. Temperature-controlled storage

Temperature-controlled storage is billable by occupied pallet day through the study close date stated in the sponsor notice. Storage after study close is billable only when the sponsor issues a written extension that identifies the protocol and storage end date.

## 5. Milestone billing

Study milestone fees require sponsor approval of the related deliverable, including kit design approval, first-patient shipment readiness, or closeout reconciliation. Milestones may not be invoiced before the approval date.
