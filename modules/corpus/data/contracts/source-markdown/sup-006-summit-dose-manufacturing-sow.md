# Statement of Work: Summit Dose Manufacturing

**Supplier ID:** sup-006
**Supplier:** Summit Dose Manufacturing
**Document Type:** Capsule manufacturing pricing and billability schedule
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER-CAP 25 mg, ALLER-CAP 50 mg

## 1. Scope of services

Summit Dose Manufacturing will provide capsule filling, encapsulation inspection, standard in-process checks, and routine cleaning for approved oral solid dose SKUs.

## 2. Unit pricing and MOQ

The unit production price is USD 0.31 per released capsule. The monthly minimum order quantity is 500,000 released capsules per SKU, but MOQ shortfall fees are not billable when aggregate sponsor-authorized released volume across same-family capsule SKUs exceeds 900,000 capsules in the production month.

## 3. Changeover rules

Product changeover is billable only when Summit changes between different product families or active ingredients. Changeover between capsule strengths within the ALLER-CAP family is included in the unit production price when performed during the same campaign week.

## 4. Cleaning validation

Routine campaign cleaning is included in unit pricing. Cleaning validation charges require an approved cleaning validation protocol and are billable only for protocol-specific validation work outside routine campaign cleaning.

## 5. Billing evidence

Each invoice must reference campaign ID, SKU, released capsule quantity, batch release status, changeover reason, and any approved cleaning validation protocol used to support non-routine charges.
