# Quality Agreement and Billing Addendum: Northstar Fill Finish

**Supplier ID:** sup-002
**Supplier:** Northstar Fill Finish
**Document Type:** Quality agreement and billability addendum
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER-INJ prefilled syringe program

## 1. Quality responsibilities

Northstar Fill Finish is responsible for sterile fill-finish operations, line clearance, visual inspection, batch documentation, and release testing support for sponsor-designated products.

## 2. Batch billability

### 2.1 Released batches

The batch manufacturing fee is billable only for batches with sponsor QA release status of `released`.

### 2.2 Rejected batches

Rejected batches are not billable as completed manufacturing. Northstar may invoice approved rework labor only when the sponsor quality lead authorizes rework in writing before the work begins.

### 2.3 Pending batches

Batches with release status of `pending` may not be invoiced for batch manufacturing, QA release, or line clearance fees.

## 3. QA release testing

The QA release testing fee is USD 7,250 per released batch and is billable once per batch. Retesting is billable only when retesting is caused by a sponsor-requested specification change or when the sponsor approves the retest in writing.

## 4. Deviation investigation

Deviation investigation charges require both a deviation record and sponsor quality approval. If the deviation is caused by Northstar process error, the investigation charge is not billable to the sponsor.

## 5. Invoice evidence

Each invoice must reference the batch ID, line clearance record, release testing record, QA release date, and any sponsor approval used to support rework, retesting, or deviation investigation charges.
