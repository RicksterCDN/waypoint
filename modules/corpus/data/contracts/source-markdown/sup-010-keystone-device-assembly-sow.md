# Statement of Work: Keystone Device Assembly

**Supplier ID:** sup-010
**Supplier:** Keystone Device Assembly
**Document Type:** Combination device assembly and inspection statement of work
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER auto-injector assembly

## 1. Scope of services

Keystone Device Assembly will provide component receipt, combination device assembly, final inspection, packaging support, and approved engineering change implementation.

## 2. Assembly billability

Device assembly fees are billable only for units that pass final inspection and are accepted in the sponsor receipt record. Failed inspection units, quarantined units, or units awaiting material review board disposition may not be billed as accepted units.

## 3. Component scrap allowance

The unit assembly price includes component scrap up to 1.5% of sponsor-issued components per monthly campaign. Scrap above 1.5% is billable only when the scrap record shows sponsor-caused component defect or sponsor-approved process change.

## 4. Engineering change orders

Engineering change order labor is billable only after the sponsor approves the ECO number, affected component, effective build lot, and not-to-exceed amount. Work performed before ECO approval is not separately billable.

## 5. Invoice evidence

Each invoice must reference build lot, accepted unit count, final inspection result, component receipt, scrap report, and any approved ECO used to support engineering change charges.
