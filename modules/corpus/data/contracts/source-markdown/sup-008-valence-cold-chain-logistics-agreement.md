# Cold Chain Logistics Agreement: Valence Cold Chain Logistics

**Supplier ID:** sup-008
**Supplier:** Valence Cold Chain Logistics
**Document Type:** Temperature-controlled logistics and lane pricing agreement
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER commercial and clinical temperature-controlled shipments

## 1. Scope of services

Valence Cold Chain Logistics will provide approved temperature-controlled transport, shipment monitoring, customs brokerage coordination, and excursion support for sponsor-authorized lanes.

## 2. Approved lane pricing

Shipment charges must use the contracted rate for the approved origin-destination lane on the purchase order. Lane surcharge, remote-area surcharge, or alternate routing charges are billable only when the sponsor logistics lead approves the route exception before pickup.

## 3. Excursion handling

Excursion handling fees require a temperature excursion record showing the shipment ID, excursion interval, temperature range, and investigation disposition. Monitoring alerts that remain within accepted temperature range are not billable as excursions.

## 4. Customs brokerage

Customs brokerage is billable once per shipment where Valence acts as broker of record. Brokerage may not be billed on both the master shipment and child shipment records for the same customs entry.

## 5. Invoice evidence

Each invoice must reference shipment ID, lane, pickup date, delivery confirmation, temperature log, customs entry number when applicable, and any sponsor approval for route exceptions or surcharge charges.
