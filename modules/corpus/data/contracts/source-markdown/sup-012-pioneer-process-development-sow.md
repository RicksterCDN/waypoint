# Statement of Work: Pioneer Process Development

**Supplier ID:** sup-012
**Supplier:** Pioneer Process Development
**Document Type:** Tech transfer and process validation statement of work
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER process development and validation program

## 1. Scope of services

Pioneer Process Development will provide tech transfer support, scale-up planning, engineering batch execution, process validation support, and protocol authoring.

## 2. Deliverable acceptance

Tech transfer and protocol authoring fees are billable only after sponsor acceptance of the named deliverable in the statement of work. Drafts, comment responses, and internal review versions are included unless the sponsor approves an added deliverable.

## 3. Engineering batch classification

Engineering batch work is billed at the engineering batch rate and may not be invoiced as a GMP validation batch unless the approved protocol identifies the batch as GMP validation and sponsor QA accepts the executed batch record.

## 4. Process validation milestones

Process validation milestone fees require completion of the milestone activity, sponsor approval of the validation protocol or report, and evidence that prerequisite engineering batches have reached the required status.

## 5. Billing evidence

Each invoice must reference milestone ID, protocol or report deliverable, engineering batch status, sponsor acceptance date, and any approved scope change used to support additional work.
