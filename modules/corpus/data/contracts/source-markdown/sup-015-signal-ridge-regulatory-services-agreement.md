# Regulatory Services Agreement: Signal Ridge Regulatory Services

**Supplier ID:** sup-015
**Supplier:** Signal Ridge Regulatory Services
**Document Type:** Regulatory submission support and translation agreement
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER global regulatory support program

## 1. Scope of services

Signal Ridge Regulatory Services will provide site addition packet support, variation submission support, health authority response preparation, and approved regulatory document translation.

## 2. Country scope authorization

Site addition and variation submission fees are billable only for countries listed in the approved regulatory work order. Work for added countries requires sponsor regulatory approval before the work begins.

## 3. Submission evidence

Variation submission milestones may be invoiced only after the submission package is filed with the applicable authority and the filing evidence identifies the country, procedure, and submission date.

## 4. Authority response milestones

Regulatory response milestones are billable only after the authority acknowledgement or deficiency letter is received and the sponsor accepts the response package for that authority interaction.

## 5. Translation rates

Document translation is billable at the contracted per-word rate by language pair. Rush translation premiums require written sponsor approval and may not be applied to sponsor-requested corrections to prior Signal Ridge translation errors.
