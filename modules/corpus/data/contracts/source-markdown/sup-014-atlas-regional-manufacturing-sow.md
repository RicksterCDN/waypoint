# Regional Manufacturing Statement of Work: Atlas Regional Manufacturing

**Supplier ID:** sup-014
**Supplier:** Atlas Regional Manufacturing
**Document Type:** Regional manufacturing statement of work
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Region:** Latin America
**Products:** ALLER-10 tablets, ALLER-20 tablets, regional OTC bundles

## 1. Scope

Atlas Regional Manufacturing will provide regional manufacturing, local packaging, and release support for sponsor-approved OTC products distributed in Latin America.

## 2. Regional price tiers

The base production price is USD 0.39 per released tablet for monthly released volume below 2,000,000 tablets.

Monthly released volume of 2,000,000 tablets or more qualifies for the regional high-volume price of USD 0.36 per released tablet for all released units in that month.

## 3. Capacity reservation

The sponsor may reserve regional capacity by month. Capacity reservation charges are billable only for months listed in the active purchase order. If the sponsor cancels reserved capacity at least 45 calendar days before the first scheduled run, the reservation charge is not billable.

## 4. Volume true-up

Volume true-ups must compare forecast volume to actual released volume for the same month and SKU. Prior-month forecasts may not be reused when updated forecasts were issued before production.

## 5. Regional packaging

Regional packaging fees are billable only for packaging formats listed in the purchase order. Artwork revision, relabeling, or market-specific carton charges require written sponsor approval.

## 6. Invoice evidence

Invoices must reference the purchase order, SKU, released batch IDs, actual released volume, active forecast version, capacity reservation month, and packaging authorization.
