# Sterilization Services Agreement: LumaSterile Services

**Supplier ID:** sup-011
**Supplier:** LumaSterile Services
**Document Type:** Sterilization and release documentation billing agreement
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER device and packaging sterilization lots

## 1. Scope of services

LumaSterile Services will provide sterilization cycles, environmental monitoring, bioburden testing, and release documentation for sponsor-designated lots.

## 2. Sterilization cycle billing

Sterilization cycle fees are billable only for cycles documented in the approved cycle record and tied to a sponsor batch or lot number. Repeat cycles are billable only when repeat processing is caused by sponsor-requested load changes or sponsor-approved reprocessing.

## 3. Bioburden and retesting

Routine bioburden testing is billable once per lot. Retesting is billable only when the original result is invalid due to sponsor-provided sample condition or when the sponsor quality lead approves a retest trigger in writing.

## 4. Release packets

Release packet fees may be invoiced only after the packet is complete and sponsor QA accepts the sterilization documentation. Draft, incomplete, or rejected packets are not billable as final release packets.

## 5. Invoice evidence

Each invoice must reference lot number, cycle record, environmental monitoring result, bioburden result, release packet ID, sponsor QA acceptance date, and any approved reprocessing or retest authorization.
