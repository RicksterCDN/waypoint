# Capacity Reservation Agreement: BluePeak Biologics

**Supplier ID:** sup-009
**Supplier:** BluePeak Biologics
**Document Type:** Biologics capacity reservation and manufacturing agreement
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Program:** BP-ALLER biologic drug substance program

## 1. Reserved capacity

The sponsor reserves 18,000 bioreactor hours per quarter. The quarterly minimum capacity fee is USD 1,250,000 and covers up to 12,000 utilized hours.

## 2. Utilized capacity

Utilized capacity above 12,000 hours is billable at USD 118 per bioreactor hour. Utilized capacity must be supported by manufacturing run logs and batch records.

## 3. Unused capacity

Unused capacity fees may not exceed the quarterly minimum capacity fee. Any unused capacity credit issued in a prior month must be applied before calculating a quarter-end true-up.

## 4. Contamination and deviation costs

If contamination or deviation is caused by BluePeak process error, investigation, cleaning, and restart charges are not billable to the sponsor. If contamination or deviation is caused by sponsor-supplied material, charges are billable only after sponsor quality approval.

## 5. True-up calculation

Quarterly capacity true-ups must use actual utilized hours from run logs. Forecast hours may be used for planning but may not be used as the basis for invoice true-ups.

## 6. Invoice evidence

Capacity invoices must include reserved hours, utilized hours, prior credits, run log references, batch IDs, deviation records, and the true-up calculation worksheet.
