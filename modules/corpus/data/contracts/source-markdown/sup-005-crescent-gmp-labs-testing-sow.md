# Statement of Work: Crescent GMP Labs

**Supplier ID:** sup-005
**Supplier:** Crescent GMP Labs
**Document Type:** Release testing and stability study statement of work
**Effective Period:** 2026-07-01 through 2027-06-30
**Products:** ALLER stability and release testing program

## 1. Scope of services

Crescent GMP Labs will perform sponsor-authorized release testing, stability pulls, method validation, certificate preparation, and related GMP laboratory documentation.

## 2. Release testing billability

Release testing is billable only after the assigned protocol is approved and the sample receipt is logged by Crescent. A release testing package may be invoiced once per batch unless the sponsor quality lead approves supplemental testing in writing.

## 3. Stability study windows

Stability pull charges are billable only for pulls completed within the study schedule window or within a sponsor-approved deviation window. Early pulls, missed pulls, or replacement pulls caused by Crescent handling error are not separately billable.

## 4. Method validation milestones

Method validation fees are milestone-based and may be invoiced only after sponsor acceptance of the validation protocol deliverable and the final validation report deliverable listed in the purchase order.

## 5. Included documentation

One certificate of analysis and one electronic release testing packet are included in each billable release testing package. Duplicate certificates, reissued packets, or format conversions are billable only when requested by the sponsor after final issuance.
